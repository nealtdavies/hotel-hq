# Hotel HQ image guide

Every picture is optional. If a file is missing the game shows a neutral placeholder, so you can add images gradually.
Put files in the folders below with the **exact filenames**. JPG only, any size (the game scales them).

**Style prefix for every prompt:** Photorealistic, bright natural light, modern luxury beachfront resort in the same style as the hotel exterior (white architecture, palms, turquoise sea, soft pastel blue and cream tones), professional photography, no text or logos, no watermarks.

| Type | Folder | Suggested shape |
|---|---|---|
| Hotel exterior | assets/hotel | wide, 16:9 |
| Rooms | assets/rooms | landscape, 16:10 |
| Staff headshots | assets/staff | square, face centred, head and shoulders |
| Scenarios | assets/scenes | wide, 16:9 (the middle strip is shown; keep the subject centred) |

You can also upload photos inside the game for any room or team member. Those override these files on that device.

## Guest faces (96 images)

Shown when a guest phones, in the guest list and on bookings. Each person always gets the same face, chosen from their name. Square, face centred, head and shoulders, a friendly look, relaxed holiday clothes, a hotel lobby or garden softly blurred behind. Generate them as grids of 6 across by 4 down with a thin plain white line between faces, and cut them with `python3 tools/split-quad.py <grid> guests <names> --grid 6x4`.

- Grown-ups: `assets/guests/f01.jpg` to `f36.jpg` (women) and `m01.jpg` to `m36.jpg` (men). A grid holds 12 women (the top two rows) then 12 men (the bottom two rows), of different ages, hair, skin tones and clothes. 3 grids.
- Children: `assets/guests/kf01.jpg` to `kf12.jpg` (girls) and `km01.jpg` to `km12.jpg` (boys), aged about 6 to 12. One grid: 12 girls (the top two rows) then 12 boys (the bottom two rows).
